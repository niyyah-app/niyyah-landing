On an app for marriage, the first question is a simple one: is this person who they say they are? A profile can be written by anyone, and a photo can be taken from anywhere. That is why Niyyah has a face check, and why a profile carries the Verified badge only after it passes.

## Why there is a check at all

A fake profile on an ordinary app wastes someone's evening. On an app where people talk about marriage, families and their future, it can do real harm. The face check is offered at the end of sign-up. You can postpone it and still use Niyyah, but without it you have no Verified badge, members who choose to see only verified profiles will not see you, and your stories wait for a moderator before they appear.

## How the check works

1. **First, a profile photo.** The check compares your face with your photos, so at least one has to be there before it starts.
2. **A three-second selfie.** You put your face in the oval on the screen and look straight into the camera. The app helps you along the way: closer, further back, a little more light.
3. **The comparison.** The selfie is compared automatically with the photos on your profile. A clear match is approved straight away. Anything that is not clear, such as a lower similarity, an estimated age under 18 or a technical error, is decided by a person from our team, usually within 24 hours. You can keep using the app while you wait.
4. **The badge.** When the check passes, your profile is marked as verified.

Before you enter Discover you also confirm your phone number or email address.

## When the check does not pass

The app tells you why, so you know what to fix:

- the face was not clearly visible, or the light was too weak
- the face did not match the profile photos
- the capture was a photo of a photo, or of a screen, instead of a live recording
- the face was covered by sunglasses, a mask or a cap
- someone else was in the frame

After several failed attempts in a row the app asks you to take a short break, and the number of attempts per day is limited. That protects the check itself: nobody can try their luck a hundred times.

## What happens to the selfie

The selfie is used only for the check. **It is never shown on your profile**, and no member can see it. It is stored encrypted, and only while your account carries the Verified badge, so that a later check has something to compare with. It is deleted when the check is rejected, when the badge is removed, or when you delete your account.

The automatic comparison is done by Amazon Rekognition in Frankfurt, in the EU. Amazon does not keep the photos and does not use them to improve its services. Because a face is sensitive data, the check rests on your explicit consent. All of this is in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Photos you upload

Every profile photo goes through a check of its own:

- blurry and very small photos are turned away
- hidden data in the image, such as GPS location, is removed

## Who sees your photos

A photo is required so that accounts cannot be invented. Showing it to strangers is not. In settings you choose:

- **Visible:** everyone on Niyyah sees your photos.
- **Blurred:** others see a blurred version until you open your photos to them.
- **Hidden:** nobody sees them until you open them.

A match does not open your photos on its own. You open them to one person at a time, when you decide to.

## What the badge does not say

Verified means one thing: the person in the photos is the person using the profile. It does not say anything about their character, their intentions or their faith. For that there are conversations, questions before chat, and family. If something feels wrong, report the profile: reports are read by real moderators who can suspend and ban profiles, and a banned account cannot simply come back with a new email address.
